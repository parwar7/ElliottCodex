# HUMAN-ASSISTED-RECURSIVE-CANDIDATE-COMPOSITION-V1

This additive logical baseline records same-process composition of one exact,
already-analyzed bounded parent candidate and an exact ordered tuple of bounded
or recursively composed child results. The relationship is a caller-supplied
structure declaration. Composition is project analysis infrastructure governed
by project operational policy; it is not Elliott methodology.

`RecursiveCandidateCompositionRequest` retains the exact parent result, ordered
child result identities, `OrderedChildBinding`, request ID, and provenance.
`RecursiveCandidateCompositionResult` retains those objects plus exact bounded
recursive nodes, the existing child aggregation result, the parent/child
operational summary, every unresolved reason, and every genuine structural
invalidity certificate reference.

The binding parent must be the parent result's exact subject. Binding children
must be the exact child-result subjects in exact tuple order. Mappings, ducks,
subclasses, serialized lookalikes, duplicates, identity cycles, missing or extra
children, and reordered children fail closed.

The existing bounded-recursion infrastructure gained two non-methodology
current-scope operational states so reviewed and otherwise-unresolved bounded
results can be represented without inventing family authority. Its existing
aggregator now inspects explicitly supplied descendants with unchanged
invalid-before-unresolved precedence. This was the proven contract gap needed
to reuse `BoundedRecursiveAnalysisNode`; no methodology or certificate contract
changed.

The three composition summaries are
`BLOCKED_BY_STRUCTURAL_INVALIDITY`,
`BLOCKED_BY_UNRESOLVED_DESCENDANT`, and
`RECURSIVE_SCOPE_OPERATIONALLY_REVIEWED`. The reviewed state means only that
the parent current supplied scope and every explicitly supplied descendant are
operationally reviewed. It does not mean valid, family-valid, complete, correct,
preferred, or tradable. Zero children are vacuously operationally resolved and
do not establish terminality or completion.

No pivot or candidate discovery, wave labeling, pattern or family inference,
degree or timeframe inference, indicator logic, ranking, trading logic,
serialization authority, family certificate issuance, or structural
certificate issuance was added.
