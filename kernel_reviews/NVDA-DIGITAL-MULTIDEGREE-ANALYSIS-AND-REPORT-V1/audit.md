# Additive implementation and analytical audit

## Scope and source authority

New executable code is Runtime orchestration/reporting, not a new Elliott behavior. `normal_component_exploration.py` delegates candidate generation, competing sets, the existing five-slot family bridge, Normal Impulse P004/P005 evaluation and recursive partial composition through public APIs. It does not duplicate either comparison or change a Kernel contract.

The bridge's ending-diagonal cardinality selector is transport into the already-approved five-slot Normal Impulse pathway. It is not a diagonal-family conclusion. The new component search is caller-supplied partial exploration of existing roles 1/3/5; it does not fabricate a source-derived internal-family requirement certificate. Its original Normal Impulse parent, original role/binding, child subject, finer snapshot and scoped original bars remain bound. The partial recursive composition has an explicitly partial singleton child binding to the same parent subject, alongside (not replacing) the unchanged original five-slot binding. Omitted siblings remain unresolved.

Source references and classifications are in `source_refs.json`. Protected definition/guideline support for proposed organization does not establish any NVDA endpoint. Candidate scopes, region choices, geometry widths, nonzero alternating movements and search caps are project operational policies. There is no hidden significance score, ranking or confidence. No source silence becomes a rule.

## Identity, numerical and safety audit

- Both new live transports pin issuance-time object identities. Later nested substitution/mutation does not refresh evidence; repeated rejection is tested.
- Parent and child evaluations use the existing repaired Normal Impulse validator. P005 operands remain exact represented prices from the original snapshot and field; no stringified-decimal substitution, epsilon or fresh arithmetic was added.
- Geometry is cached by exact observation/config identity and strict parent interval. Cached observations, bars and discovery digest are checked before reuse. Subject/result authority is not cached across alternatives.
- Finer observations are explicitly selected; no resampling or timeframe/degree substitution. Timestamp containment is not asserted to prove coincident orthodox endpoints or full child coverage.
- JSON export cannot recreate live authority. Exact IDs in a report are provenance, not certificates. New transports refuse pickle restoration.
- Scope generation is finite: at most 10,000 bars per component, consecutive six-pivot windows, first/last eligible scope only; aggregate 24 searches / 64 hypotheses. Actual 24 processed searches, 25 hypotheses. Exclusions are nonfatal and unvisited scopes are explicit.
- Existing P004-invalid hypotheses are visibly rejected. Seven contextual hypotheses retain fatal P004 even with P005 sufficiency. No rejection of one alternative validates another or proves family impossibility.
- No provider calls, network retrieval, credentials, chart-control actions, execution logic, indicators calculation, ranking, forecasts or trading were added. Imported existing experiment modules are reused for public types and utilities; their Yahoo retrieval paths are not invoked.
- Runtime source has no filesystem write API. New runner/report helper writes new outputs beneath validated Runtime paths only. All protected access is read-only. Historical files are not overwritten.

## Analytical limitations found, not repaired

1. **Aggregate timestamp authority:** Monthly high 236.54 belongs to the May monthly bar labelled May 1, while the Daily extremum is May 14. The existing strict parent interval was not extended; equality of price does not confer endpoint identity.
2. **Region-selection limitation:** the macro proposal's 2009 window selects a local November 2009 low, not the entire 2008–2009 minimum. Old omitted interiors prevent presenting M1/M2 as a complete historical count. No outcome-driven retuning occurred.
3. **Search breadth:** all 333 Monthly bars were geometrically inspected at widths 2 and 8, but only 7 regional roots and 2 declared macro proposals were evaluated. Eligible unvisited windows, cross-region exclusions and prefixes are not proof of absence.
4. **Actual depth:** Monthly → Weekly → 4H links reach two child edges. Daily windows with 2/5/4 pivots did not supply six-point hypotheses. The same exact parent role was retried at finer resolution under a separately saved pre-evaluation retry plan, not through an invented intermediate parent. Missing 1H/15m historical coverage and caps stopped other paths.
5. **Current position:** M1 offers a conditional post-May interpretation; M2 leaves a potentially continuing role open. Forming geometry in M2 remains ineligible for resolved P005. Neither identifies a certified active wave. The 229.56 Daily quote is a partial captured bar, not live price or session close.
6. **Indicators:** saved daily study values were matched by exact timestamp and plot column, not recomputed. Their capture is later than the OHLCV snapshot and is explicitly kept separate. No divergence, momentum confirmation or volume-wave rule was inferred.
7. **Visual QA limitation:** canonical portable HTML validation/package and semantic fallback checks passed. An installed Chrome attempt failed the builder's environment check. Compatible headless-shell was unavailable. Chart label coordinates are reconciled numerically, but rendered label placement and click interactions are not claimed visually verified. No browser was installed and no bespoke replacement chart framework was introduced.
